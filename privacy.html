Сайт работает и без аккаунта. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

1. Overview

This page explains what data is used on the PixelCore Studio website. Our apps are covered in section 6.

2. What data is collected

The site works without an account. If you create one we store: your email (the password is protected by Firebase Authentication and we cannot see it), username and avatar, favorite games, reviews and comments (public, shown under your username), and bug reports and suggestions (visible to the administrator only). The name, email and message you send through the contact form are visible to the administrator only.

3. Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). The site uses no advertising or third-party analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google servers. If reCAPTCHA-based Firebase App Check is enabled to reduce fake requests, Google performs that verification. The site is hosted on Vercel. To detect Telegram Mini App mode, pages load Telegram's official script from telegram.org (including in a regular browser), so your browser contacts telegram.org. When the site is opened inside Telegram, the Telegram app passes settings such as language and theme to the site; signing in with a Telegram account is not used at this stage, and no Telegram data is stored in our database.

4. Browser storage and cookies

Your browser stores your language, favorites list, a download marker, your cookie choice and whether you closed the services promo window. If you allow "statistics", a general visit counter is incremented once per session (no personal data). You can change this via "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of the page.

5. Deleting your data

The "Delete account" button on your profile page removes your account, reviews, comments, bug reports and suggestions. To remove messages sent through the contact form, write to the address below.

6. Our apps

Our apps and games do not collect, store or share any personal data with third parties. They use no advertising or analytics tools.
